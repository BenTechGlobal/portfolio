My Transition into Cybersecurity

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB), graduated in 2023, where my academic training spanned abstract problem-solving, analytical reasoning, and a final-year prerequisite in Data Science with Python libraries. Alongside my degree, I developed practical software development capabilities—building and deploying dependency-free web applications in HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript and sharpening my engineering discipline through texts like Eloquent JavaScript.

In late 2025, recognising how rapidly artificial intelligence is reshaping software engineering and expanding the digital attack surface, I made a deliberate strategic transition into cybersecurity. Guided by the principle of building breadth before narrowing into a single niche, I structured a comprehensive hands-on roadmap across 15 cybersecurity specialisations—starting with Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC), where human judgement, organisational context, and executive decision-making remain irreplaceable even in an AI-accelerated era.

I approach cybersecurity honestly and methodically: I do not claim senior commercial years I have not yet lived. Instead, I bring verified foundational credentials, rigorous exam preparation, real-world teaching and data-collection discipline, and an executable portfolio of simulated enterprise labs designed to demonstrate how I think, document, and solve security problems.
